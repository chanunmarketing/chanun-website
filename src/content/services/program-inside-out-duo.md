  **What the research says**

  - A study in the Annals of Internal Medicine (2004) found that intravenous vitamin C produced far higher blood levels than oral doses, which level off as the dose rises.
  - A randomised split-face trial published in 2021 found that inflamed acne fell significantly more on the side treated with cold plasma, with no serious side effects.
  - Vitamin C is a cofactor for the enzymes that build collagen, which is why caring from within supports skin repair.

  **Before you come**

  The doctor reviews your history before every session. If you don't have recent blood results, a blood panel is recommended first. Please eat normally and drink water beforehand.

  **During and after**

  - Radiance and Clear: done together, with the drip running while your mask rests
  - Active: plasma on the back first, then the drip
  - You may feel coolness along the vein during the drip, and the skin may look slightly pink after plasma for a few hours
  - Use sunscreen every day and carry on with your day as normal
